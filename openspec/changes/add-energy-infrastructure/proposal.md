# Proposal: add-energy-infrastructure

## Why

The economy currently has one investment decision: spend gold on kill power (towers) or hoard it for interest. Adding an energy layer — towers consume energy, bought from an infinite grid at a flat tariff or generated by solar panels built in the maze — creates a second, slower investment axis that rewards long-term planning: a panel is treasury converted into a permanent bill reduction, weighed against the interest that gold would have earned and the tower it could have bought. It mirrors the real household-electricity problem (pay the utility forever, or invest in capacity up front), which is a deliberate design goal: the player should come away with an intuition for energy ROI.

## What Changes

- Towers gain an energy demand: a constant idle load while a wave runs, plus a per-shot cost when they fire (hybrid model). Demand scales with upgrade level.
- Solar panels become a new buildable structure: 1×1 footprint, blocks pathing like a wall, produces a constant energy rate while a wave is running. No enemy interaction (panels cannot be attacked, drained, or stolen from).
- The grid supplies unlimited energy at a flat gold-per-unit tariff. Each tick, demand not covered by solar is bought automatically from the treasury. Excess solar is wasted (no storage, no sell-back).
- Brownout: when the treasury is at or below zero, grid purchase stops (extends the existing no-spending-below-zero rule) and towers degrade proportionally to the uncovered share of demand. Energy joins the death-spiral: theft → broke → weakened towers → more theft.
- All energy flow (production, billing, brownout) runs only during waves, mirroring interest — the untimed build phase must not accrue per-tick effects.
- HUD shows the energy balance: current demand, solar supply, net bill per second.
- Balance and level schemas gain energy fields (tower demand, panel stats, tariff).

Non-goals of this change (candidate future changes, listed so they are not accidentally rediscovered): energy storage/batteries, selling back to the grid, capacitor-powered burst attacks, harvesting energy from enemies, variable solar output (weather/day-night), enemies damaging or draining panels, player-set energy budgets.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `energy-system`: Tower energy demand, solar generation, automatic grid billing against the treasury, and brownout degradation when the treasury cannot pay. Covers the panel as a buildable, path-blocking structure and the wave-only accrual rule.

## Impact

- **Sim** (`src/sim/`): `tower.ts` (demand, degraded fire rate), `economy.ts` (billing step in the tick order), `placement.ts` + `grid.ts` (panel as a placeable, blocking structure with the standard removal delay), `types.ts`, `commands.ts` (build-panel command).
- **Data** (`src/data/`): `schema.ts` and `balance.json` (tower `idleLoad`/`shotCost`, panel `cost`/`output`, per-level scaling), level schema (`tariff` under `economy`).
- **Render/UI** (`src/render/`, `src/ui/`): panel mesh, build-palette entry, HUD energy readout, brownout indicator on towers.
- **Determinism**: all energy quantities are integers (milli-energy, matching milli-gold); billing joins the fixed tick order; the replay-hash test golden changes.
- **Balance risk** (flagged for playtesting): a panel is functionally a wall that pays dividends — walls stay relevant only if panels cost significantly more; brownout compounding the death spiral may need tuning against the existing −100 bankruptcy rule.
